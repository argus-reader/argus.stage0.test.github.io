Reader Study

This is a research project by the Centre for Eye Research Australia (CERA) and approved by the Human Research Ethics Committee at the Royal Victorian Eye and Ear Hospital (INSERT).

You will initially complete a short survey and an educational enrolment package (estimated 20 minutes). Following this, you will assess retinal scans for glaucoma damage (30 seconds per case). Unless explicitly instructed, you can stop and resume anytime, your responses save automatically, just log off the platform during breaks.

Your responses are stored against a unique study identifier, not your name. Your email is used only for login and registration, stored separately to your responses. Your AHPRA registration number is collected and used only for authorisation purposes. All data is held on a secure CERA server in Australia and is managed in accordance with relevant privacy requirements. Fully de-identified data is kept for 15 years and then destroyed. De-identified data only will be shared with research collaborators in Australia and internationally. There are no anticipated risks in these reader sessions, which offer educational benefit through active participation.

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time without giving a reason. Responses recorded up to that point may still be used in the study analysis in a de-identified form. Published de-identified results cannot be withdrawn.

By registering below, you confirm you have read the above, agree to take part in ARGUS reader sessions and understand that de-identified responses will be used for research.

Thank you kindly for your support.
